# Operator guide — LSO

Portal: gov.ls e-Services directory / Ministry of Finance and
Development Planning's Procurement department (supplier registration +
procurement-plan publication) — the Public Procurement Regulations,
2007 (LesLII, lesotholii.org) is the named legal basis; its own
detailed section text could not be independently read this iteration
(Cloudflare bot-challenge on LesLII's individual document pages, see
`src/marketentry/facts.cljk`).

Entity: Companies Register (Ministry of Trade & Industry,
companies.org.ls) business/company registration, One-Stop Business
Facilitation Centre (OBFC). TIN registration (Form 1) is filed as part
of company registration itself. Engagements seeking a Contractors
Registration Certificate also clear gov.ls's own published
category-fee schedule (A=2000/B=1500/C=500/D=300) before a filing can
proceed. Human-gated filing only.
